Welcome to the Degrading Web

This is a demonstration of digital decay. When you first arrived, everything looked pristine and readable. But as time passes, the very fabric of this website begins to deteriorate.

The fonts become harder to read. The letters start to space out. The text begins to blur and skew. Even the background slowly fades to darkness. This is not a bug – it's a feature.

"All things must pass, even websites. The impermanence of digital spaces mirrors the transient nature of our online existence."

You might wonder why someone would create such a thing. Perhaps it's a commentary on how our digital experiences often degrade over time. Or maybe it's just an experiment in unconventional web design.

The degradation is subtle at first – you might not notice it for the first 30 seconds. But stay longer, and you'll witness the slow dissolution of readability. The question is: how long will you stay?

This effect works by tracking your time on the page and progressively applying CSS transformations. The longer you stay, the more intense the degradation becomes. It's irreversible within this session.
