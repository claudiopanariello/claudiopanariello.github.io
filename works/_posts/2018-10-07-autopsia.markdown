Written for [<highlink>Divertimento Ensemble</highlink>][divertimento] for the International Workshop for Young Composers IV edition.<br>
Instrumentation:  B.Fl., Ob., B.Cl., Perc., Piano, 2 Vn., Vla, Vc.<br>
Prémiere: 14 July 2018 Auditorium Santa Chiara, Bobbio. Dvertimento Ensemble, Sandro Gorli cond.<br>
Duration: ca. 8 min. <br>
<br>


*“Look at your body:<br>
A painted puppet, a poor toy<br>
Of jointed parts ready to collapse,<br>
A diseased and suffering thing<br>
With a head full of false imaginings.”<br>
The Dhammapada*<br>

I found almost by chance these lines while reading a book by Thomas Ligotti and the image was so strong to induce me to investigate this mechanical (and suffering) nature of the human being, realizing a musical autopsy: the body/piece, gradually assembled and then dissected, shows its internal structure made up of imperfect gears and repeated modules and reveals itself as a machine, now turned off.
